# Project Context: HCM Lite

**Objective:** Build an AI-assisted Human Capital Management (HR) system from scratch for a hackathon. The system serves Admins, HR, and Employees. It starts completely empty with no dummy data; everything must be generated organically through user actions (setup, registration, daily use).

## The "Why" - Context for the AI Assistant
The AI Assistant (Module 8) cannot exist in a vacuum. It relies heavily on the constraints, roles, and data generated by the other 8 modules. The AI is a conversational layer *on top of* the existing system rules and permissions, acting as an interpreter and assistant rather than a direct database manipulator.

## Mind Map: System Architecture & Modules

*   **HCM Lite Core**
    *   **Users & Roles (Strict Data Scoping)**
        *   **Admin:** Company setup, HR creation, payroll finalization, sees all data.
        *   **HR:** Employee registration, payroll drafting, attendance/leave approvals, HR dashboards.
        *   **Employee:** Self-service check-in/out, leave requests, own payslips, own data only.
    *   **Modules (M1 - M9)**
        *   **M1: Auth & Access:** JWT, 3 roles, role + data-scope guards.
        *   **M2: Organization Setup:** Departments, designations, leave types, holidays, rules.
        *   **M3: Employees:** Directory, profiles, optional CSV import/export.
        *   **M4: Attendance:** Web check-in/out, status rules (late, half-day), corrections.
        *   **M5: Leave:** Apply, balance, overlap/holiday checks, approvals.
        *   **M6: Payroll Lite:** Salary structure, monthly runs, tax slabs, payslip PDFs.
        *   **M7: Dashboards & Analytics:** KPIs, trends, and insights based on role.
        *   **M8: AI Assistant:** (Our Focus - See M8 Task Breakdown).
        *   **M9: Comms & Audit:** Announcements, notifications, strict audit logging.

## Core Business Rules (For AI Context)
*   **Office Hours:** Start 09:00, Grace 15 mins (Late after 09:15), Half-day < 5h.
*   **Leave Quotas:** Annual 14, Sick 8, Casual 10. Unpaid is unlimited.
*   **Payroll:** Gross = 1.5x basic. Deductions for absences and half-days. Prorated for joiners/leavers.
